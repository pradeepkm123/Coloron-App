Privacy Policy - ColorOn VMS

Effective Date: August 3, 2026 | Last Updated: August 3, 2026

ColorOn ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, ColorOn VMS (the "App"), available on Android and iOS platforms.

1. Overview & Purpose of the App

ColorOn VMS is a professional Video Management System (VMS) mobile application designed to enable users to view live CCTV camera streams, manage NVR/DVR/IP Camera hardware devices, control PTZ (Pan-Tilt-Zoom) functionality, review stored video playback, listen to audio streams, and send two-way audio talkback commands.

We prioritize your security and data privacy. Your video feeds, live camera streams, and stored surveillance recordings remain private to you and your connected hardware.

2. Information We Collect and App Permissions

To provide core VMS features, the App requests access to specific device capabilities:

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Used exclusively for scanning QR codes on NVR/DVR labels for device addition and taking snapshots.
  • Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Enables real-time two-way audio talkback between your mobile device and supported CCTV/NVR devices.
  • Storage & Media Access: Enables saving video clips, manual recordings, and camera snapshots directly to your device's local storage.
  • Network & Wi-Fi Access: Required to establish RTSP, WebSockets, and P2P video streaming connections with your camera hardware on your LAN or WAN.
  • Notifications Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Delivers real-time push notifications for motion detection events and alarm triggers.

3. How We Process and Use Your Information

All data processed by ColorOn VMS is used strictly for technical performance and functional operations:

  • Transmitting video and audio streams directly between your surveillance hardware and mobile device.
  • Storing device connection parameters (IP addresses, ports, device serial numbers, usernames, and encrypted credentials locally).
  • Sending motion detection and system alarm notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

4. Video Stream Privacy & Data Security

Zero Video Harvesting: ColorOn does NOT collect, store, record, analyze, or stream your camera footage to our servers. Your surveillance video remains strictly between your local hardware and your mobile device.

Local Credential Storage: All device passwords and credentials are saved directly on your mobile device in protected local storage.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or video feeds with third parties or advertisers.

Standard functional SDKs integrated:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For push notifications delivery.
  • Expo / React Native Core Modules: For application execution.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You can remove your saved camera configurations at any time by deleting devices from the App or clearing the App storage (`Settings > Apps > ColorOn > Clear Data`) / uninstalling the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The ColorOn VMS App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
